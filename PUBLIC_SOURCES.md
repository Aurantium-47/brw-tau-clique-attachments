## Bounded literature caveat
The graphs K_{a,b} minus one edge are co-interval and Ferrers/chain graphs. These connections and the sigma-polynomial literature warrant further expert comparison. The inspected sources did not identify a theorem subsuming the precise maximal-clique Bell quotient and universal attachment result presented here. That bounded observation is not a certificate of novelty or global openness. Structural separation from the explicitly specified cograph/cycle/Theta(2,2,3) seed closure is not a priority claim.
