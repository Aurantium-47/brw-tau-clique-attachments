# Classical ingredients and limited literature comparison

Date: 2026-11-02. This is an attribution update, not a novelty certificate.

## Verified primary ingredients

**E. J. Farrell and Earl Glen Whitehead, Jr.** “Connections between the
matching and chromatic polynomials,” *International Journal of Mathematics
and Mathematical Sciences* 15(4) (1992), 757–766.
[DOI 10.1155/S016117129200098X](https://doi.org/10.1155/S016117129200098X).
[Primary full text](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/HOA/IJMMS/Volume15_4/213850.pdf).

* Theorem 1 / Corollary 1.1, p. 759: triangle-free complement matching
  counts equal the chromatic falling-factorial coefficients.
* Theorem 3, p. 760: general forbidden-edge inclusion–exclusion.
* Corollary 3.2, p. 761: its rectangular complete-bipartite specialization.
* Theorem 7, pp. 761–762: matching-equivalent surrogates can have different
  vertex order, with that order accounted for in the chromatic conversion.

These are equivalent classical ingredients of the present matching-board
argument. The audited paper was not found to state the combined
Bell-transformed rising-factorial quotient/universal tau-attachment theorem.
The original PDF was inspected in the source audit; this attribution
revision also checked the corresponding local primary-text passages.
Complement bars can be lost in OCR, so the audit checked the PDF visually.

**Haicheng Ma and Yinkui Li.** “The Matching Equivalence Graphs with the
Maximum Matching Root Less than or Equal to 2,” *Applied Mathematics* 7
(2016), 920–926. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4236/am.2016.79082),
[primary full text](https://file.scirp.org/pdf/am_2016052713401572.pdf).
Lemma 3.2(2), p. 923, states
`mu(T_(1,1,n),x)=mu(P_1,x) mu(C_(n+2),x)`.
Here the three arms have lengths 1,1,n; putting n=L-2 recovers exactly
the bare cycle/fork identity used in the antecedent odd-cycle note.
The audit inspected the primary full text; this revision relies on that
verified inspection rather than claiming another independent PDF review.

## Scope of the comparison

The scoped audit inspected the above papers, BRW (1994), Wang (2021),
and the 2026 graph-join preprint, and searched cycle/fork equivalence,
matching/complement quotients, clique attachment, and reflected/paired
rooted constructions. No exact published paired-decoration equivalent was
located. That is a limited search outcome, not proof of novelty or absence.

The earlier BRW Proposition 5.13 already uses a transformed chromatic
quotient in a different sufficient clique-attachment criterion. This note
does not claim its family fails that criterion or strictly extends every
previous result. The paired deletion construction uses classical rooted
matching/deletion methods.

The Mu–Shen–Xu paper, DOI 10.1142/S179383092550185X, still lacks an
available inspected primary full text for this comparison. Its overlap
remains unresolved. Denied publisher access was not retried or bypassed;
abstract snippets do not determine theorem scope. Earlier attribution of
the fork identity exists in secondary literature, but no claim is made
here to have inspected its original earlier source.

All mathematical and computational claims retain the scope and caveats
of the research note and independent mathematical audit.
