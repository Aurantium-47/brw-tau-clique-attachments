# Public sources, existing scope, and bounded literature check

Checked 2 November 2026. This is not a priority certificate.

## Exact original target and dependencies
- Francesco Brenti, Gordon F. Royle, David G. Wagner, *Location of zeros of chromatic and related polynomials of graphs*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 46(1) (1994), 55-80. https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1994-002-3 . Primary PDF: https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8B66867EF89BFB7C22DDD36E718D8157/S0008414X00045016a.pdf/location-of-zeros-of-chromatic-and-related-polynomials-of-graphs.pdf
- Original Conjecture 6.5 is ordinary clique intersection, with separate tau and w clauses. Conjecture 6.6 concerns graph join instead.
- BRW Proposition 5.12 already proves ordinary tau-real union closure when the intersection has at most one vertex. New attachment claims here are therefore focused on edge intersections (or larger ones in the explicitly stated anchored-clique class). Do not count empty/vertex intersection as a new theorem.
- BRW Propositions 5.2/5.4 give the Bell-product root-preservation input in reflected normalization; Proposition 1.1 supplies chromatic clique amalgamation. With P_G(x)=(-1)^n chi_G(-x), B((x)_j)=z^j for falling factorials, B(P_G)(z)=(-1)^n tau_BRW(G,-z).
- BRW Proposition 5.7 uses the closed parabolic region R_T(1) to imply tau-realness. Proposition 5.13 uses R_T(0) as a sufficient attachment criterion. Neither should be substituted for the other.
- Ole J. Heilmann and Elliott H. Lieb, *Theory of monomer-dimer systems*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 25 (1972), 190-232. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01877590 . Nonnegative weighted signed matching polynomials are real-rooted; reciprocation gives the nonpositive-root matching-size reversal used here.
- Mingyang Kang, Zhixin Liu, Sophie C. C. Sun, Philip B. Zhang, *Real-rootedness of the tau-polynomial under graph joins*, arXiv:2609.07457v1. https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07457v1 . This resolves BRW Conjecture 6.6, not general 6.5, and is an acknowledged weighted matching/root-lifting precedent.

## Incomparability, Ferrers, and rook-polynomial leads
Francesco Brenti, *Expansions of chromatic polynomials and log-concavity*, Transactions AMS 332(2) (1992), 729-756, https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1992-1069745-7 , and BRW's incomparability results concern sigma-polynomial real-rootedness in the inspected statements. They must not be silently relabeled as Bell clique-quotient or arbitrary tau-factor attachment theorems.

Richard Ehrenborg and Stephanie van Willigenburg, *Enumerative properties of Ferrers graphs*, https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2918 , is relevant to chromatic enumeration, not an established substitute for the quotient argument here.

Fresh targeted searches combined tau-polynomial/chromatic with Ferrers, incomparability, permutation graphs, and clique quotient. They located these primary sources and many unrelated uses of tau, but no identified theorem subsuming the precise quotient/attachment result. The previous co-interval/rook literature check likewise found no such theorem among inspected sources. This bounded observation does not establish historical novelty or exhaustive coverage. The poset determinant identity may have earlier formulations; no priority is asserted for it or for matching root lifting.

## Frozen earlier result
The already released theorem covers K_(a,b) minus one cross-edge and a stronger-input structural operation ((G1+u) join (G2+v))-uv when B(P_Gi/x) is nonpositive-real-rooted. It does not replace that hypothesis with ordinary tau-realness. The present extension does not alter that release.
