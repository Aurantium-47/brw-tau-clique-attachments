# Classical dependency and provenance

The non-elementary input is J. Borcea, P. Brändén and T. M. Liggett, *Negative dependence and the geometry of polynomials*, Journal of the American Mathematical Society 22 (2009), 521–567, Theorem 4.20 (partial symmetrization).

Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0707.2340 (v2, Theorem 4.20 and proof, PDF pages 29–30).
Published version: https://www.ams.org/journals/jams/2009-22-02/S0894-0347-08-00618-8/S0894-0347-08-00618-8.pdf
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0894-0347-08-00618-8

The theorem allows complex multiaffine stable generating polynomials and preserves stability under a convex mixture of identity and a coordinate transposition. We fix the remaining upper-half-plane variable w, apply it only to the multiaffine y variables, and then vary w again. The proof in the primary source explicitly supports this complex specialization. It is not a claim about unrestricted convex mixtures.

The scalar certificates and actual-seed/C6 proofs are supplied in PROOF.md and independently recomputed by the included reviewers. Earlier same-project results motivated the construction but are not needed as unnamed mathematical premises. The Bell shift/product identities are proved or directly expanded in the proof. No general Weyl-symbol theorem or polarization theorem is required by the final argument.

Both independent review reports are preserved as reviewed artifacts. Their original local proof/checker path references identify provenance; the actual runnable copies are in the corresponding release subdirectories.
