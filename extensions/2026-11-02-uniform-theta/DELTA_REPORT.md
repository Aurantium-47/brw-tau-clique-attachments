# Standalone uniform-theta addendum

The accepted source proof has SHA-256 `f6f67a217f78c299259fad65244b81cc240f9c1451184b82f74dc5c49abe6a4c`. The reader addendum transcribes its uniform radius, Vieta, Newton-sum, angular-distribution and factor-grouping argument. Exact path-coloring identities, the local quartic repair lemma and the Bell-product normalization are included explicitly, so no inaccessible source note is required.

The quantifier is one unspecified integer M for all triples a,b,c >= 2 with max(a,b,c) >= M. Neither an explicit threshold nor all-theta real-rootedness is asserted. The unchanged separate two-theorem manuscript retains its valid weaker fixed-pair quantifier; this addendum strengthens it without replacing or modifying that release.

Private review narratives, coordination records, local execution paths and account identifiers are excluded. Public citations and AI-assistance limitations are retained. The existing exact algebraic checker and original receipt are copied byte-for-byte; their hashes are in CHECKER_PROVENANCE.json. The new guarded runner only orchestrates this check. Exact identities and finite sign intervals are evidence for the formulas, not a numerical verification of the analytic theorem.

This addendum has its own allowlist, manifest, checksums and separate transcription/privacy audit. No external writes or changes to earlier release files were performed in preparing it.
